Abbasid Baghdad

750–1258 CE (Abbasid Caliphate)
بَغْدَادُ العَبَّاسِيَّةُ

Baghdad became a major political and intellectual centre. Across the Abbasid period, scholars worked in translation, mathematics, medicine, astronomy, philosophy and historical writing.

711–1492 CE (Muslim rule in parts of Iberia)

Al-Andalus included Iberian territories governed by Muslim rulers at different times. Its history includes urban culture, architecture, agriculture, philosophy, medicine and complex intercultural encounters.

c. 1299–1922 CE

04 · Read history critically

How to study a civilization

A civilization is more than a dynasty or a collection of monuments. Study its people, institutions, economy, beliefs, environment and sources.

Questions worth asking

  • Who held political power, and how did administration work?
  • How did scholars, artisans and merchants contribute?
  • What evidence survives, and who produced it?
  • How did the society change over time?

Build an evidence-based account

Compare chronicles with inscriptions, coins, architecture, manuscripts and modern scholarly research. Distinguish established facts from interpretation, and acknowledge disagreement where evidence is uncertain.
