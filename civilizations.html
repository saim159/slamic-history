A brief chronological guide

Dates provide orientation; historical periods often overlap and differ by region.

750 CE

Abbasid Revolution

The Abbasids came to power, and Baghdad later became a major political and intellectual centre.

909 CE

Fatimid Caliphate

The Fatimid state emerged in North Africa and later established Cairo as its capital.

11th century

Seljuk Expansion

Seljuk political and military influence expanded across parts of the Middle East and Anatolia.

13th century

Mamluk Rule

The Mamluks established a powerful sultanate centred on Egypt and Syria.

How to study a civilization

A research-minded approach helps separate historical evidence from later legends and assumptions.

01

Start with chronology

Identify the period, rulers, major political changes, and the limits of the state's territory.

02

Examine primary sources

Look for chronicles, inscriptions, coins, buildings, manuscripts, legal texts, and contemporary accounts.

03

Compare interpretations

Compare reputable modern studies and ask which claims are supported by evidence and which remain debated.
