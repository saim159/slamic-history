Historical Timeline

Some important milestones in Islamic history.

610 CE

Beginning of Revelation

The first revelation was received by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in the Cave of Hira.

622 CE

The Hijrah

The migration from Makkah to Madinah marked a major turning point in Islamic history.

750 CE

Rise of the Abbasids

The Abbasid period became one of the most important eras of Islamic intellectual and cultural development.

1492 CE

End of Muslim Granada

Granada, the last major Muslim polity in Iberia, fell to Ferdinand and Isabella.
