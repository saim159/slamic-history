How we work

Research Before Representation.

A visual history platform must be engaging without sacrificing accuracy, transparency or respect for historical uncertainty.

◎

Traceable References

Cite the relevant work, author and edition; include volume and page when verified.

⌖

Responsible Visuals

Distinguish authentic artifacts from modern illustrations and imagined portraits. Check image licences and attribution.

⇄

Compare Perspectives

Where sources disagree, explain the disagreement rather than presenting uncertain details as settled fact.
