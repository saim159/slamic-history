Historical Timeline

Beginning of Revelation

The first revelation was received by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in the Cave of Hira.

622 CE

The Hijrah

The migration from Makkah to Madinah became a defining moment in Islamic history.

750 CE

Rise of the Abbasids

The Abbasid period became an important era of intellectual and cultural development.

1492 CE

End of Muslim Granada

The fall of Granada marked the end of Muslim political rule in most of the Iberian Peninsula.
